## 1. Context

The audit phase decides whether a cycle may ship by running, against the cycle's worktree, the exact commands CI runs (ADR-0069 :24-29): `go vet ./...` (ciparity.go:252 ↔ .github/workflows/go.yml:46), `go test -count=1 -tags acs ./acs/regression/...` (:263-264 ↔ ci.yml:57), the `-tags integration -race` tier over the touched packages (:306-309 ↔ go.yml:65 minus `-coverprofile`, plus `-p 4 -parallel 4`), `apicover -enforce` folded in-process (:657-723 ↔ go.yml:118-128) and the new-package graduation check (:737-778).
