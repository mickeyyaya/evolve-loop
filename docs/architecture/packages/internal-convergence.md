# internal/convergence

> The decision: [ADR-0126](../adr/0126-every-iterative-loop-converges-or-escalates.md). The design it implements (§2, §2.1, §3, §3.1, §3.2, §4, §6.1, §7 and §9): [convergence-policy.md](../convergence-policy.md). The plan (components V1–V3): [convergence-policy-2026-10.md](../../plans/convergence-policy-2026-10.md). The example it replays: [L2's seven review rounds](../../incidents/2026-10-07-l2-seven-review-rounds.md). The policy block: [internal-policy.md](internal-policy.md). The verb: [cmd-evolve.md](cmd-evolve.md).

## Purpose

`internal/convergence` decides the next rung of any repeat-until-accepted loop: change the feedback, then the strategy, then the scope; land the best round; never run a round past the budget. `Decide(Input) Decision` is pure: no file, clock, environment or network is read. A loop supplies its judgments `J_0..J_r` (the §2.1 schema), its fixer and judge (tier, model, effort), the headroom table of their families and the resolved `workflow.convergence` block (`policy.ConvergenceConfig`). It gets back one `Decision`: the rung, the action, the blocking bar, what the next round changes, what is deferred or filed, the round to land, and the reasons. Its first caller is the console, through `evolve convergence decide`; the pipeline loops (V5, V6, V10–V12) call it in shadow first.

## Design

- **Validate at the boundary, decide on trust.** `Parse` decodes the rounds JSON strictly (an unknown field or a second document is refused; the schema's one home is the verb's entry in [runtime-reference.md](../../operations/runtime-reference.md)), and `Input.Validate` checks the loop, the round count, each index, id, severity, status, kind, class, falsification word and hunk, refuses a CRITICAL whose status is DEFERRED or FILED (a CRITICAL is never deferred or filed, so such a row is malformed), and checks the config with `policy.ConvergenceConfig.Validate`, the policy package's one home for the bar words and the config ranges. `Decide` assumes a validated input: the verb validates before deciding (exit 10), and a pipeline loop validates at its call site, where a malformed input keeps the loop's current behaviour (design §10).
- **The ladder is replayed, never stored.** `newLadder` recomputes the rung of every completed round from `J_0..J_r`, so the bar in force for round *r* and "was there a rung-1 round" need no extra input, and the same rounds always give the same decision. `rungAfter(k)` applies, in order: `J_0` takes the schedule; a round at rung 2 is the last fix (rung 3 follows, cause `final-round`); oscillation jumps to rung 3; spent marginal gain skips to rung 2, except after a round marked `reentry` (rule 9); otherwise the schedule by *N* (`schedule`: rung 2 at *N*, rung 1 from round 2, rung 0 for round 1), so `max_fix_rounds` is enforced by construction. The ladder keeps each round's rung and the cause that put it there.
- **One rules row per loop** (`rulesByLoop`, data rather than branches). code-review and console-lane use everything: verify-only judges, fixer and judge raises, fresh context, the raised bar, deferral, filing by rule, accept, adjudication; code-review's split is a Stop plus a continuation and console-lane's unstages. audit-repair and explanation-reauthor use the judge raise, the fresh context and Stop only, at the base bar, with no deferral, filing, split or accept (the audit keeps its verdict rules, design §6). cycle (§6.1) raises the fixer, takes a fresh context at rung 2 but keeps its base bar (it defers nothing, so a raised bar would land past an open MEDIUM unrecorded), stops at rung 3, and takes *N* from `max_backward_edges`; inbox-item raises the fixer, takes a fresh context and splits or stops; ship-recovery only stops at its bound. Every other *N* is `max_fix_rounds`, which a caller sets to its own budget (the envelope's 2, `TaskRetryCeiling`, `maxRecoveryDepth`).
- **Strict open.** A finding blocks at bar *B* when it is OPEN, at or above *B*, not filed by rule and not in a component a split or accept exempted; a CRITICAL is never exempted (`exempted`). DISPUTED never blocks: it goes to the adjudicator (§4.5). Filing by rule (code-review and console-lane only): INFO; a capability below CRITICAL; a late finding below HIGH; a late CRITICAL or HIGH only when its `falsification` is `refuted`. An absent `falsification` means the check has not run, so a late CRITICAL or HIGH without one blocks (fail-safe). A CRITICAL capability is a defect and meets the late rule like any other.
- **Land on the next round's bar.** The land check uses the bar of the round that would come next, so a loop whose next round would raise the bar to HIGH lands now when nothing at HIGH remains, with its MEDIUM and LOW deferred (output first, design §5). A land's `Rung` is the last round's rung, or 2 when the landing used rung 2's raised bar, and its `CONVERGENCE_RUNG` names that rung's own cause (`landRung`): the L2 replay lands at rung 2 with `cause=schedule`, the cause that made round 3 its final round, not the `final-round` cause of the rung 3 that never ran.
- **Keep-best** (`view.keepBest`, `rankOf`): rounds rank by the fewest strict findings at the decision's bar, then by the lowest open mass at LOW and above (the default weights), then the earliest, with the exempted components left out (never a CRITICAL). It is computed for every decision (for a Continue it is the checkpoint to keep), and a terminal decision's deferrals and filings come from the kept round's own judgment, never automatically from the last. Every landing (`landing`: Land, Split, AcceptWithLimits) re-checks the round it lands and Stops if that round holds an open CRITICAL.
- **Repair damage and progress** (`gainOf`): repairs are findings whose latest earlier status was OPEN and are FIXED now; damage is a FIXED finding reopened, plus a first-seen OPEN finding whose `file:line` lies inside the round's `fix_hunks` (inclusive). Both count LOW and above. Progress compares the mass of round *r*−1 and round *r* at the bar of round *r*, so a bar raise cannot fake it. A round marked `reentry` (an audit-repair re-entry, rule 9) skips the whole marginal-gain test, damage and progress alike, and signals neither, while the round budget keeps counting.
- **Mass.** The default weights are CRITICAL 8, HIGH 4, MEDIUM 2, LOW 1, INFO 0 over unresolved findings at the bar. A loop may supply `MassFunc(round, atBar)` (code-review's U(n) adds its gap mass); it sees only the unresolved findings at the bar.
- **Concentration** (`view.concentration`): over the `concentration_window` judgments ending at *r*, the OPEN findings at LOW or above with a known component, each id once; defined only with at least `concentration_min_findings` of them and only for a full window. It fires when the windows ending at *r* and *r*−1 both reach the threshold on the same component (a different top component in each window is not one hot spot), ties going to the lexically smallest component. Before rung 3, the hot component takes its own exit when it holds a strict finding and no CRITICAL is open: console-lane and inbox-item split it if separable, code-review and console-lane accept it if certified fail-safe, otherwise the signal and the redesign follow-up (`Decision.Redesign`) are all that happen. A cycle loop's split is a Stop, so code-review never splits early, and concentration alone never stops a loop.
- **Rung 3 exits** (`exitScope`), in order: adjudication at the judge family's top, when HIGH findings remain, no CRITICAL is open and the table has headroom; split, when one separable component holds every strict finding; accept with limits, when every strict finding's component carries a fail-safe certificate; otherwise Stop, which files every OPEN finding of the kept round. Split and accept both refuse beside an open CRITICAL.
- **Oscillation** (`oscillates`): an id reopened after FIXED twice (ADR-0124's "FIXED claim rejected twice"), or one failure `fingerprint` reported by two judgments whose backward `edge` differs (§6.1). A judgment's `fingerprint` is the failure it reports and its `edge` the backward edge that failure takes, so the decision after it sees the edge about to be taken. Repeats over the same edge are the inner loop's own retries (audit repair's second attempt), which keep their budget.
- **Headroom** (`HeadroomTable`): a raise goes one canonical tier up (`modelcatalog.CanonicalTiers`) to the next tier the family's table names; adjudication goes to `top`. A raise counts only when the table's (model, effort) for the target differs both from the table's entry for the current tier and from the pair the fixer or judge actually ran at (its own `model` and `effort`, each falling back to the table's when absent), within the same family. The feedback change is made once: the fixer and judge raises go with the first rung-1 round, or with rung 2 when no rung-1 round ran (`feedbackChangeDue`). A family or tier the table lacks has no headroom. A raise that changes nothing returns a zero `TierEffort` and the reason `CONVERGENCE_NO_HEADROOM`, never a faked raise. The judge is raised only for a reasoning-class blocker (`class`: correctness, concurrency, architecture).
- **Reasons are returned beside the values, never collected as a side effect.** The decider is an immutable value: each step that builds a `Decision` puts its own reasons in `Decision.Reasons` (a missed raise returns its `NO_HEADROOM` line with the zero `TierEffort`; `settle` adds `FILED`, `SPLIT`, `ACCEPTED_LIMITS` and `DEFERRED`; `stop` adds `SPLIT` and `STOP`), and `Decide` puts `CONVERGENCE_RUNG loop= round= rung= bar= action= cause=` first, then the judged round's `NO_PROGRESS` and `REPAIR_DAMAGE`, then `CONCENTRATION`. The order is pinned by `TestReasons_FollowTheRungInAFixedOrder`. Registering the codes as signals is V8.

## Invariants

- **No round past the budget.** `TestDecide_MaxFixRoundsIsNeverExceeded` (every *N* from 1 to 4 and four rounds past it) and `TestDecide_Rule8_TheRungsFollowMaxFixRounds` (the rung table for *N* = 1..5).
- **A CRITICAL is never deferred and never lands.** Deferral covers only [LOW, bar); split and accept refuse beside an open CRITICAL in the judged round; a split or accept never exempts a CRITICAL, so keep-best never lands an earlier round that holds one (the hole the review's probes 01 and 02 found, where accept-with-limits landed round 0 and deferred its open CRITICAL); every landing re-checks the landed round; a CRITICAL capability blocks; a late CRITICAL whose falsification has not run blocks; a DEFERRED or FILED CRITICAL is refused by `Validate`: `TestExit_SplitAndAcceptBothRefuseWithAnOpenCritical`, `TestConcentration_AnOpenCriticalRefusesTheEarlyExits`, `TestExit_AcceptDefersTheLandedRoundsResidueAndNeverAnEarlierCritical`, `TestConcentration_AnEarlyAcceptLandsARoundWithoutTheCritical`, `TestCapability_BelowCriticalIsFiledAtOnceAndACriticalIsADefect`, `TestLate_ACriticalOrHighWhoseFalsificationNeverRanBlocks`, `TestValidate_RefusesMalformedRoundsUnderTheirCause`.
- **No audit finding is deferred.** `TestReplay_AuditRepairWithTheEnvelopeBudgetOfTwo` checks every decision of the replay.
- **The L2 replay lands at round 3 through rung 2** (`TestReplay_L2LandsAtRoundThreeThroughRungTwo`, fixture `testdata/l2-rounds.json`): the bar is HIGH, the 1 MEDIUM and 4 LOW are deferred, the INFO is filed, and concentration (0.636, then 0.647, INFO excluded) fires on `bridge:model-check` with nothing strict left, so it signals and files the redesign follow-up.
  - **How the fixture was reconstructed.** Per finding, from the four round tables in `cli-routing-table-2026-10.md` ("L2 fix round", "L2 final round", "L2 fix round 6", "L2 fix round 7"): `J_0` is the fix round's review, `J_1` the final round's delta check, `J_2` round 6's and `J_3` round 7's. - **A cycle never crawls to the crash guard.** `TestReplay_ACycleBouncingOnOneFingerprintStopsByTheThirdBackwardEdge` (audit → build twice, then retro → tdd on one fingerprint: Stop at the third edge, even with a 32-edge budget) and `TestReplay_ACycleNeverCrawlsToTheCrashGuard`.

## Findings

- **The default LOW and INFO weights are unobservable.** Progress is measured at the bar, and every bar is MEDIUM or above, so a mutant that changes LOW's 1 or INFO's 0 is equivalent. The weights stay as the design's table; INFO's weight 0 shows where it can, in repair damage and concentration, which both exclude it.
- **The landed-round CRITICAL re-check is an equivalent mutant today.** Removing it (`F-C1b` in the fix-round mutation run) changes no decision, because a split or accept never exempts a CRITICAL and every landing path first refuses an open CRITICAL in the judged round, so keep-best can only land a round with none. It stays as the console's defense in depth for the one invariant that must never break.
- **Today no family has headroom.** Every manifest's `model_tier_map` names the same model for deep and top and carries no effort, so the verb's L2 replay at round 1 reports `CONVERGENCE_NO_HEADROOM` for both the fixer and the judge. V3b (Claude top = Opus 5.5 at xhigh) gives the tier table an effort per tier; the verb must then read it into `ModelEffort.Effort`.
