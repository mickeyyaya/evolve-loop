# Option 2 — Three cards: landing, delivered state, resume

Citations: [assumptions-and-evidence.md](../assumptions-and-evidence.md).

## What changes

Each acceptance criterion gets its own card and its own review round. The landing card covers `EnforceActor`, F3, F11 and F12. The state card covers F2 and Finding 3. The resume card covers Findings 4 and 5.

## Causal chain to the goal

Smaller cards give smaller reviews. Each review can start when its card is done (E7 shows three review rounds on the first card).

## Expected effect

- 7 of 7 inputs decided, spread over 3 documents (E1).
- The `delivered` state and the landing share a contract (E3, E4). Split cards must repeat it, or link it, so the risk of two versions of one rule grows.

## Cost and time to effect

Three document cycles and three review rounds. The inbox item names one card (A4). No time was measured.

## Risks and early detection

1. **Two cards disagree on `delivered`.** Detection: the review of the second card.
2. **The item is closed on the first card.** Detection: the inbox acceptance check at close (A4).
